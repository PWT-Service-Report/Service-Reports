Privacy Policy

Krenos™ Water Management System · Last updated 29 October 2026

Krenos is a field reporting tool used by water treatment service providers. It is not open to public sign-up: every account is created by the operating company for one of its own engineers. This policy explains what the system stores and why.

What we collect

  • Engineer account details — name, work email and an internal engineer code, entered by the operating company.
  • Service report content — water analysis readings, chemical dosages and stock levels, observations and recommendations, entered by the engineer during a site visit.
  • Client details — company name, site region and the contact address the report is sent to.
  • Visit location — approximate GPS coordinates recorded when a report is submitted, used to confirm the visit took place on site. Location is visible only to the operating company's management and never appears in the report or the emails.

Krenos collects no advertising identifiers, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and builds no profile of its users beyond the above.

How it is used

The data is used solely to produce the service report, deliver it to the people entitled to receive it, and keep a history so trends across visits can be compared. It is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Where it is stored

  • Supabase (Frankfurt, EU) — accounts and report records, in a database where each engineer can read only their own work.
  • Google Drive — the generated PDF reports, in the operating company's own Google account, filed by client.
  • Resend — used to deliver the report emails.

Google user data

Krenos requests access to Google Drive for a single purpose: to upload the PDF report it has just generated into the operating company's own Drive, in a folder named after the client, and to produce a link so the recipients can open it.

Krenos does not read, download, index, or analyse any other file in that Drive. Google user data obtained through this access is not transferred to any third party, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train any model. Access can be withdrawn at any time by the account owner at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who can see a report

A finished report goes to three places: the client contact, the engineer who carried out the visit, and the operating company's management. Within the system, an engineer sees only their own clients and their own reports; management sees all of them.

How long it is kept

Service reports are business records and are kept for as long as the operating company needs them. An engineer account is deactivated when the person leaves, which immediately ends their access while leaving the reports they filed intact.

Your rights

If you are a client whose site appears in these reports, or an engineer with an account, you may ask for a copy of the data held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted where no legal or contractual obligation requires it to be kept. Write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page is updated and the operating company notifies affected account holders.
