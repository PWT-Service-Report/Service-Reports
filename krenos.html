Water Management System

Krenos is a field reporting tool for industrial water treatment engineers. An engineer visits a site, records the water analysis on a phone or tablet, and the system produces the service report and delivers it to the client, the engineer and the office.

What it covers

  • Boilers, cooling towers, closed loops, raw water and swimming pools
  • Parameter limits checked as the readings are entered
  • Cycles of concentration, condensate return and LSI calculated automatically
  • Chemical stock tracked against dosage, with a warning before a client runs out
  • Report history and trends across previous visits
  • PDF reports in English and Arabic

Who uses it

Krenos is operated by water treatment service providers for their own engineers. It is not a public sign-up service — accounts are created by the operating company for its own staff.
