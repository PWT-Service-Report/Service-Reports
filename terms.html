Krenos™ Water Management System · Last updated 29 October 2026

What this is

Krenos is a private field reporting tool. Accounts are issued by the operating company to its own engineers. There is no public registration, and the system is not offered to the general public.

Using an account

  • An account belongs to one named engineer and is not to be shared.
  • Readings and observations entered must reflect the actual site visit.
  • Client data seen through the system is confidential and may not be copied or passed on outside the operating company.
  • The operating company may deactivate an account at any time, which immediately ends access.

Reports

A submitted report is a business record. Engineers cannot alter or delete a report once it has been filed; corrections are made by management, so the record of what was reported stays intact.

Technical advice

Calculated figures and recommendations produced by the system are decision support for a qualified engineer, not a substitute for professional judgement. Treatment decisions remain the responsibility of the engineer and the operating company.

Availability

Krenos depends on third-party services for hosting, storage and email delivery. It is provided as-is, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability.
